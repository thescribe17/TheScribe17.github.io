Over the past little while, my website has been undergoing a complete rebuild — new structure, new layout, new navigation, and a much cleaner way to explore everything I write. It’s been a big job behind the scenes, but I’m delighted to say the new version of **The Desk of Karen Lee Field** is now live. Although it has a long way to go before it's finished.

As things change, you’ll notice clearer pages, easier navigation, and a more organised home for my books, blog posts, interviews, reviews, and writing resources. My goal is to create a space that feels welcoming, simple to use, and enjoyable to explore, whether you’re a long‑time reader or visiting for the first time.

Thank you for your patience while things are being shuffled around. Your support means a great deal, and I’m excited to share more updates, stories, and writing news with you in the weeks ahead.

If you spot anything odd or have suggestions, feel free to reach out through the Contact page, when it's working. Otherwise, enjoy watching the new site change and grow — I hope it feels like a comfortable place to settle in and read.
