This month I’m delighted to share an interview with Australian author **Pamela Freeman**, whose work spans children’s fiction, young adult fantasy, and adult epic fantasy.

Pamela discusses her writing process, the inspiration behind her stories, and the challenges of building rich, believable worlds. Her insights into character development and research are especially valuable for new writers.

A wonderful conversation with a talented storyteller.
