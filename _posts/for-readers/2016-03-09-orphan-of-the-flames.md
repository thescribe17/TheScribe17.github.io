**Rating:** ★★★★☆

*The Orphan of the Flames* continues the Secret Breakers series with another clever, code‑cracking adventure. Brodie, Hunter, and Tusia return to Station X, where they face a new puzzle connected to the mysterious Voynich Manuscript.

This instalment blends history, mystery, and teamwork in a way that keeps young readers turning pages. The stakes are higher, the clues more intricate, and the friendships stronger than ever.

A great read for anyone who enjoys secret messages, historical mysteries, and fast‑paced adventure.
