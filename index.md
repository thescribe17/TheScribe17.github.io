# The Desk of Karen Lee Field

Welcome to my corner of the writing world — a place where stories grow, mysteries unfold, and imagination is always welcome. I write fantasy adventure and middle‑grade mystery fiction, and this website is where I share my books, my writing journey, and the things I discover along the way.

Whether you’re a writer seeking guidance, a reader looking for your next favourite book, or simply someone who enjoys a good story, you’ll find something here to explore.

## Explore the Site

- **[For Writers](/for-writers)** — resources, tips, and writing‑related blog posts.
- **[For Readers](/for-readers)** — author interviews and book reviews.
- **[Archives](/archives)** — browse posts by year.

Thank you for visiting. I hope you enjoy your time at the desk.
